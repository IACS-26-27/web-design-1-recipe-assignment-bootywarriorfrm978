How to make a steak grilled cheese sandwhich

Ingredients

  • - stick of butter
  • -steak (of your choice)
  • -bread (sourdough works best)
  • -cheese (Cooper sharp American works best)
  • -fresh thyme or rosemary
  • -garlic cloves

    • Step 1 -Prep for grilling

    put a decent amount of butter one a pan and let it melt as the pan heats up and prep your bread

    Step 2- Prep steak

    Take out the steak from the fridge and let it thaw out for 10 to 20 minutes or until it becomes room tempature then pat dry with a paper towel

    Step 3 -Start Cooking steak

    -Turn pan on high (til 125-130 degrees F) and let it warm up to make a crust on the steak.

    -After crust is made turn stove to meduim heat, and drop 2 tablespoons of butter, a couple smashed garlic cloves, and fresh thyme or rosemary into the pan and with a large spoon contiusoly baste the steak with the butter for 1-2 minutes and then take out steak when tempature turns down 5 degrees.

    Step 4 -Rest steak and make grilled cheese

    -Put steak on the side. -heat up pan and spread butter across bread (preferably sour dough).

    -now with the buttered side of bread face it on the pan add cheese of your liking on to the bread(Cooper Sharp American works best) and pan fry bread till golden brown

    -before adding your top peice of bread cut the steak in pieces of your liking and put it on top of the cheese.

    -now butter another peice of bread and put it on top and flip the whole sandwhich and make sure the only sides of the bread touching the pan is the ones with butter on it.

    Step 5- plate food

    Now that you have your gilled cheese sandwhich cut it into 2 trianlgular pieces and serve
