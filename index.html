How to make a steak grilled cheese

Step 1 -Prep for grilling

put a decent amount of butter one a pan and let it melt as the pan heats up and prep your bread

Step 2- Prep steak

Take out the steak from the fridge and let it thaw out for 10 to 20 minutes or until it becomes room tempature then pat dry with a paper towel

Step 3 -Start Cooking steak

-Turn pan on high (til 125-130 degrees F) and let it warm up to make a crust on the steak.

-After crust is made turn stove to meduim heat, and drop 2 tablespoons of butter, a couple smashed garlic cloves, and fresh thyme or rosemary into the pan and with a large spoon contiusoly baste the steak with the butter for 1-2 minutes and then take out steak when tempature turns down 5 degrees.

Step 4 -Rest steak and warm up pan for grilled cheese
